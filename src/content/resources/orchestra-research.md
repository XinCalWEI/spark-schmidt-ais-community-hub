Orchestra is one of the AI-assisted research tools under consideration for conceptual and hands-on discussion in the SPARK program.

Listing a tool here does not imply endorsement or confirm a forum session. Participants should evaluate research claims, privacy implications, costs, data handling, and reproducibility before using any AI platform in their work.

## Responsible use questions

- What research data leave the local environment?
- Can intermediate decisions and generated artifacts be inspected?
- How are uncertainty, failure, and human oversight documented?
- Can another researcher reproduce the workflow without the same service?
