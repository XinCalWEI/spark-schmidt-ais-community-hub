The SPARK Forum is being designed as a connected journey from understanding real-world needs to building responsible, reproducible research artifacts.

The Forum will take place March 8–12, 2027, in Ann Arbor, Michigan. These dates are confirmed. March 7 is reserved for participant check-in only, before the main program begins. The specific venue, participant support arrangements, and speakers are still being finalized. Please wait for venue, lodging, and support details before booking travel.

The current program concept includes expert-led discussions, accessible introductions to emerging AI methods, hands-on technical training, interdisciplinary project work, mentorship, and informal community activities.

Applications are open. Visit the [participation page](../../forum/participate/) to open the Google Forms application or scan the application QR code.
