The SPARK Forum is being designed as a connected journey from understanding real-world needs to building responsible, reproducible research artifacts.

The Forum is tentatively planned for March 7–12, 2027, in Ann Arbor, Michigan. March 7 is reserved for participant check-in only; the main program runs March 8–12. Dates remain tentative, and the specific venue, participation details, and speakers are still being finalized. Please do not make travel arrangements until the dates and remaining logistics are confirmed.

The current program concept includes expert-led discussions, accessible introductions to emerging AI methods, hands-on technical training, interdisciplinary project work, mentorship, and informal community activities.

Applications are not yet open. Please stay tuned for application details.
