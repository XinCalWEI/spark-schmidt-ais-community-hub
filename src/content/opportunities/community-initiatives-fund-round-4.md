## Who can apply

This call is available to **current and former Fellows of the Eric and Wendy Schmidt AI in Science Fellowship Program** who want to host a community initiative.

## Funding and purpose

Awards of up to **US $100,000** are available for community initiatives or events. Proposals should bring together current Fellows, alumni, and—where useful—other guests and experts to explore important but neglected questions related to applying AI and machine-learning approaches in science.

An initiative may be an event, such as a workshop, retreat, or conference. It may also take another collaborative, community-oriented form.

Each initiative should advance at least two of these objectives:

- Generate new insights, practical guidance, or further questions concerning shared challenges.
- Create new research collaborations and/or communities of shared practice and learning.
- Deliver meaningful skills development related to applying AI/ML tools in science.
